Why it exists

Less guesswork. More useful runs.

Uma Musume asks players to balance ratings, skills, course conditions, stamina, support cards, and scenario resources. UmaTools brings those choices into one consistent workspace, with the underlying logic documented alongside the calculators.

Practical by design

Compare options quickly, understand the result, and move straight back into your training run.

Private by default

No account is needed. Supported saves remain in your browser, and sharing stays in your control.

Open about the math

Detailed documentation explains scoring models, assumptions, limitations, and how each tool reaches its answer.
